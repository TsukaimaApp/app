Setup

You bring the Hermes.

Tsukaima has no server of its own. It needs a Hermes instance your phone can reach, on a LAN, a tailnet or behind your own proxy.

Enter your server's address.

Sign in with your Hermes dashboard username and password. Plain http is accepted for local and private hosts. Anything public needs https.

Pick a profile and a model.

Each Hermes profile keeps its own chats. The chip in the top bar changes the model in the middle of a conversation.

Send something.

The line under the top bar reports what the agent is doing. When it wants to run something that needs your say, a card asks first.

Why does it fail on Sundays?

Thought for 12s

The job runs at 03:00. Sunday is also when the weekly snapshot runs, so both may want the same lock.

The weekly snapshot still holds the lock at 03:00 on Sundays. Moving the backup to 04:30 clears it.

Self-hosted

Your server is the only one.

The app talks to one Hermes instance and nothing else. There is no Tsukaima backend, no analytics and no sync. Your username and password sit in Android's encrypted storage and are sent only to your Hermes.

FAQ

Before you install.

Do I need a Hermes instance?

Yes. Tsukaima is a client. It needs a running Hermes Agent to connect to, on a home server, a VPS or a laptop.

Can I reach it from outside my network?

The same way you reach Hermes: Tailscale, WireGuard, or a reverse proxy with TLS. Plain http is allowed only for loopback, LAN, tailnet and single-label hosts.

Does anything go to Nous or to the author?

No. There is no telemetry. Images from other hosts load only when you tap them. Phone voice uses Android's speech services. Live voice, if your Hermes runs it, streams audio to OpenAI with the key held on your server.

What is kept on the phone?

Your sign-in, in encrypted storage. A copy of each chat you have opened, your drafts and any message waiting to send, in the app's private files. Signing out deletes all of it.

Which tool calls need approval?

The ones your Hermes asks about. Its settings decide; the app shows every request and never answers one for you.

Does it keep working in the background?

While Android lets it. A foreground service holds the connection for notifications, and Android 15 caps that at about six hours a day. After that the app is quiet until you open it.

How do I get updates?

Add it to Obtainium, which watches the GitHub releases, or download the next APK yourself. The app does not update itself.

Which devices?

Android 10 and newer on a 64-bit ARM processor: phones, foldables and tablets.

iOS?

Not planned.

Why the name?

A tsukaima (使い魔) is a familiar: a spirit bound to its master and sent out to do the work. The agent runs somewhere else. This is the part of it you carry.
